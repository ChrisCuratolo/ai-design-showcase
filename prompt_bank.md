# Prompt Bank (examples)

This is a small, curated list of prompt templates for escalation and incident communications. Use and adapt during interviews to demonstrate process and artifacts.

1) Customer acknowledgement (friendly):
"Hi <Customer>, we’ve detected an issue impacting <service>. Our team is investigating and will update you within 2 hours. Could you confirm any recent changes on your side?"

2) Customer acknowledgement (concise):
"We’re investigating an outage affecting <service>. We’ll update within 2 hours. Please share any recent changes."

3) Internal status note (for warroom):
"Summary: service <X> error rate ↑50% starting 10:35 UTC. Impact: payment processing failures for 10% of transactions. Current mitigation: traffic routed to fallback cluster. Next steps: reproduce, identify faulty deploy, plan rollback if needed. Owner: @eng-oncall"
