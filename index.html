Case Study — Prompt Bank & Escalation Templates

Role: AI Design Lead for Escalations

Problem: Agent messages were inconsistent and overly verbose during incidents, increasing customer confusion.

Actions: Created a prompt bank of templates, ran A/B mock tests, and introduced a simple evaluation rubric focused on conciseness and clarity.

Outcome: Decreased irrelevant assistant content by ~35% and shortened response length by ~20% in controlled tests; agent time-to-first-response decreased slightly due to improved templates.
